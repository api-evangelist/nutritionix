Nutritionix operates the world's largest verified nutrition database, exposing a Track API (v2) that converts natural-language food and exercise descriptions into full nutrient analysis, and powers food search, branded item lookup, and restaurant menu data for diet, fitness, and health applications.

**Areas:** Restaurant · Health · Nutrition · Food · Fitness · Public APIs · Food and Beverage

- [Nutritionix Brands API](https://apis.io/apis/nutritionix/nutritionix-brands-api/) — Search the database of food and restaurant brands.

- [Nutritionix Natural Language API](https://apis.io/apis/nutritionix/nutritionix-natural-language-api/) — Translate plain-text food and exercise phrases into structured nutrition data.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Nutritionix: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
